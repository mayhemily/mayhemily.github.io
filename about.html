Emily Szpiro is a PhD student at UCLA and a graduate intern in the publications department at the Getty Research Institute. She previously worked as an editorial fellow at the LA Review of Books. Her work considers how modernist authors such as Virginia Woolf, H.D., and Gertrude Stein conceptualize the porousness and sensitivity of the sick body at the intersection of its environment and, in doing so, explore the potential of the novel's experimental aesthetics.
